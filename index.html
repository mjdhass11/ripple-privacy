LANTERN REACH

Privacy policy · last updated 24 September 2026

Lantern Reach is a single-player puzzle game — it appears on your device under the name Ripple. It is designed to work entirely on your device and to collect nothing about you. The same text is in the app itself, under Settings → Privacy policy.

What the game stores

Your progress — stars, streaks, chosen theme and settings such as sound, vibration and accessibility options — is saved in the app's own storage on your device. So is a count of attempts, wins and time per level, so that you can show a playtest report if you are testing the game for us. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can erase all of it with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

What the game does not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No personal information, contacts, location, photos or files are accessed.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising software is included.
  • No data is shared with us or with anyone else. The game makes no network requests at all.

Permissions

The Android app asks for Vibration, for the gentle haptic taps when a lantern is pushed or reaches its lotus, and Internet, a standard permission for the web view the game runs inside. The game makes no network requests of its own.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and, as described above, collects no personal information from anyone.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the game stores stays on your device for as long as the app is installed, and we never receive a copy of it — there is no server to send it to. Settings → Reset progress erases it; uninstalling the app removes it with the app. Because the game has no accounts, there is no account to delete and nothing held anywhere else.

Changes and contact

If the game ever changes what it stores or shares — for example if online features or advertising are added — this policy will be updated first and the date above will change.
